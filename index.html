Research standards

Methodology & transparency

Every polling record should identify the polling organization, field dates, population surveyed, sample size, survey mode, question wording where available, source, and retrieval date.

Polls are snapshots of opinion during a defined period—not guarantees or predictions. Compare state polling with that state's actual result. Do not treat missing data as zero, infer missing state polls, or present an average as an individual pollster's result.

Historical state-level polling is not available for every election. Clearly label unavailable years and jurisdictions. Check source terms and obtain permission before republishing restricted polling data.
